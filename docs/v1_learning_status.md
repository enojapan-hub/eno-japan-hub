# ENO JAPAN V1 — Quality Status

Priority: working learning flows over feature count.

Required before calling V1 complete:
- Belajar cards open real database content and never remain static demo-only cards.
- Kanji includes readings, meaning, audio/TTS, related vocabulary, example sentences, and practice.
- Kotoba includes reading, meaning, forms, examples, audio/TTS, related Kanji, and practice.
- Bunpō is structured as pattern, meaning, structure, usage, multiple examples, comparison, practice, and explanation.
- Retention and spaced repetition are persistent per member.
- Profile loads member identity and progress reliably after Google OAuth.
- Free plan is useful; social follow can grant 14 days Premium once per platform; referral/share rewards are persisted.
- JLPT countdown uses configured official test dates.
- Official JLPT announcements are surfaced without inventing dates.
- All user-facing labels are Indonesian by default; Japanese terms remain where educationally appropriate.
- Every primary button must have a working route/action.

Do not declare a feature complete merely because its UI exists. Verify the data path and action path end-to-end.
