# ENO JAPAN N1 — Final consolidation

N1 consolidation manifest covering high-level vocabulary, idiomatic and formal expressions, complex grammar, dense informational and argumentative texts, implicit meaning, author position, cross-reference, and advanced listening inference.

The N1 curriculum should measure the official N1 ability target: understanding Japanese used in a variety of circumstances. The current official 2026 test structure is Language Knowledge (Vocabulary/Grammar) + Reading for 110 minutes and Listening for 55 minutes.
