# ENO JAPAN — Reference Curriculum Policy

## Purpose
ENO JAPAN may use widely used Japanese-learning textbooks as curriculum references to improve coverage and sequencing at every JLPT level.

## Reference families
- 日本語総まとめ (Nihongo So-Matome): vocabulary, grammar, kanji, reading/listening progression by JLPT level.
- 新完全マスター (Shin Kanzen Master): higher-detail JLPT grammar, reading, listening and kanji/vocabulary progression.
- TRY! 日本語能力試験: grammar-centered progression with contextual examples and practice.
- みんなの日本語 (Minna no Nihongo): foundational classroom vocabulary, grammar sequencing and communicative situations, especially N5/N4.
- Other widely used Japanese-learning references may be added after verification.

## Copyright rule
These books are references, not sources for copying. ENO JAPAN content must be independently authored. Do not reproduce textbook pages, answer keys, passages, audio scripts, distinctive exercises, or substantial verbatim lists. Use references to identify concepts, coverage gaps, sequencing and commonly taught vocabulary/grammar, then write original explanations, examples and exercises.

## Per-level integration
N5: prioritize Minna no Nihongo-style foundational progression plus So-Matome/TRY coverage.
N4: expand everyday vocabulary, connective grammar, practical reading/listening and kanji progression using multiple references.
N3: combine broad vocabulary/kanji coverage with Shin Kanzen Master-style skill separation and TRY/So-Matome grammar progression.
N2: emphasize nuanced grammar, formal vocabulary, reading strategies, collocations and listening inference.
N1: emphasize advanced vocabulary, kanji compounds, formal/written grammar, discourse, long reading and high-level listening inference.

## Database fields
Each curriculum item should record level, category, topic, source_reference_tags, learning_objective, explanation, original_examples, difficulty, prerequisites, related_items and review_priority.

## Source/reference tags
Use tags such as `minna_no_nihongo`, `so_matome`, `shin_kanzen_master`, `try_jlpt`, and `other_verified_reference`. Tags describe curriculum provenance/inspiration and do not imply copied text.

## Quality gate
Before publication: deduplicate, verify level placement, verify Japanese naturalness, check kanji readings, check example accuracy, ensure exercises are original, and ensure no copyrighted passage/audio/script has been reproduced.
